This graduate certificate program provides students with an interdisciplinary understanding of how technology, especially artificial intelligence (AI), intersects with law, legal practice, and governance. Participants will gain the skills to evaluate, implement, and manage AI solutions while addressing ethical, legal, and policy considerations.

  1. Program Objectives
    • Understanding the intersection of law and technology
    • Proficiency in legal software and tools
    • Knowledge of data privacy and cybersecurity laws
    • Skills in legal research and document automation
  2. Program Structure
    • Introduction to Legal Technology: Overview of technology in the legal field
    • Legal Software and Tools: Training on software like case management systems, e-discovery tools, and contract analysis software
    • Data Privacy and Cybersecurity: Laws and best practices for protecting client data
    • Legal Research and Document Automation: Using technology to streamline legal research and document creation
    • Blockchain and Smart Contracts: Understanding blockchain technology and its application in legal contexts
    • Ethics in Legal Technology: Ethical considerations and professional responsibility in using technology in legal practice
  3. Delivery Method
    error Students are expected to attend in-person classes. Some classes may have in-person assessments, even though the course delivery is remote or hybrid!
    • In-Person: Traditional classroom setting
    • Online: Fully online courses, either synchronous (live) or asynchronous (self-paced)
    • Hybrid: A combination of in-person and online learning
  4. Program Length
    • Graduate certificates are completed in eight months over two semesters (Fall, from September to December, and the following Winter, from January to April)
  5. Day Program
    • Courses for this certificate are offered during the day, with classes and mandatory evaluations scheduled anytime between 8 am and 5 pm, Monday to Friday
  6. Industry Partnerships
    • Collaborate with legal tech companies and law firms to provide practical experience and networking opportunities for students. Guest lectures, internships, and project-based learning can enhance the program
  7. Accreditation and Approval
    • Ensure the program meets the accreditation standards and get approval from the relevant academic bodies at Seneca Polytechnic
  8. Marketing and Recruitment
    • Develop a marketing plan to attract students. Highlight the demand for legal tech skills and the career opportunities available to graduates
